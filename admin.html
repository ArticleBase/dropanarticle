Writer's desk

Create a travel story

Use TinyMCE to format a useful, original article. Draft autosave is local until you save it to Firebase.

TinyMCE Editor

Lowercase words separated by hyphens. Keep published slugs stable where possible.

Use an image you own or have permission to publish. Inline image uploads in TinyMCE require editor permissions.

Separate relevant terms with commas.

Aim for 140–240 characters where possible.

Use H2/H3 headings, meaningful internal links, helpful lists, image alt text, and original experience or research. Avoid thin promotional copy.
